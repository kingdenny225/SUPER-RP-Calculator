Predicted BCR-free survival · 0–36 months
Research tool — not for clinical decision-making. This calculator reproduces a single-centre Cox model with internal (bootstrap) validation only and no external validation. It applies only to prostate-cancer patients undergoing radical prostatectomy with post-hormone pathology after prior ADT. The 5-variable (+PSAD) option is a sensitivity analysis fit on the smaller subset with available prostate volume (n = 284) and in head-to-head testing adds no measurable discrimination over the 4-variable model (ΔC = +0.001, LR p = 0.42). Estimates are probabilities for a group of similar patients, not certainties for one person, and become less precise beyond 24 months (few patients remained at risk by 36 months). Do not use it to guide treatment.
